Wallet Overview

Initialize Wallet

Choose a wallet mode. This is a one-time choice and cannot be changed later.

Paper wallet
Simulated wallet with a made-up SOL balance. Trades are simulated with a static fee. No real transactions.
Shadow paper wallet
Made-up SOL balance, but sizing and fees come from a real (un-executed) transaction. Nothing is actually traded.
Live
Uses your real wallet and executes real trades. Displays the actual wallet balance.
Set the total SOL to allocate for trading, the static per-trade fee, the per-minute grace period, and the finalization lag (how many minutes behind real time the most recent usable candle is — e.g. 5 makes the simulation act as if 5 minutes ago is the latest candle). Defaults are prefilled from the current configuration.
Shadow mode trades a made-up SOL balance but quotes every buy/sell against the real market at decision time (via Jupiter), recording the shares and price impact a live trade would get. It never signs or submits — only the public key is used, so no funds can move. Fees come from the live quote, so there are no static-fee or grace-period settings.
